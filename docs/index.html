Support & FAQ

How do I add the widget to my iPhone Home Screen?

  • Open the Locker Room? app, tap the Gear icon (Settings), and add your team(s).
  • Long-press an empty area on your iPhone Home Screen until the icons jiggle, then tap Edit / + in the top-left corner.
  • Search for Locker Room?, choose Small, Medium, or Large, and tap Add Widget.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Locker Room? does not collect, store, track, or share any personal information, analytics, location data, or user identifiers.

  • No Account Required: The app requires no sign-in or personal details.
  • On-Device Preferences Only: Your selected hockey facilities and team names are stored solely on your device (in local app and widget storage) to display your games.
  • Public Schedule Data: The app fetches publicly posted daily game schedules and locker room assignments directly from TimeToScore.
